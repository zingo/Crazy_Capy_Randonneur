Cache / render cost benchmark (T20-mini)

Equal operation rate (10 ops/s), 400 operations per sample, samples spread across the route; screen on/off; lower is better.

  • maptiles — the map library's tile cache. render = emptied, tiles fetched/downloaded during the test; pre-cache = already downloaded and stored.
  • turnpreview — the small map picture at a turn. render = generated now, on the spot; pre-cache = generated earlier (the app's “Pre-cache” action), only decoded and shown now.

Why this matters: on a real ride the energy saved per preview is small next to the display, which dominates total draw. The larger practical wins of pre-caching are much less CPU work and far fewer network/tile fetches (a quieter radio, less data, and it keeps working offline) — which is what the per-operation numbers below show.
