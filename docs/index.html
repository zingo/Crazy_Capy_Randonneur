Crazy Capy Randonneur — docs

Voice-first, battery-extreme bike navigation. Most of what's worth documenting here is how little power the thing can get away with.

Power

The app is measured on real hardware over repeatable ghost rides and recorded at ~1 kHz from a USB power meter. The result is a committed power baseline — colour-coded by level and device, click a column to sort — regenerated from the runs kept in data/power/<tag>/.

How it's measured — the meter, the harness, presets, warm-up, charging trimming, and reproducibility — lives in the power-measurement toolkit docs.
